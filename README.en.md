An open-source prompt for researching PhD supervisors, checking programmes and funding, drafting outreach emails, and tracking applications.

Spend around **15 minutes a day** reviewing research, email drafts and next actions. Initial setup, reading, writing proposals and submitting applications can take longer.

## What it covers

- Research supervisors and show concrete evidence of fit.
- Check programmes, eligibility, deadlines and funding against official sources.
- Draft individual outreach emails for your review.
- Track opportunities, conversations, materials and next steps.

The prompt contains 30 stages, from onboarding through research, outreach and application preparation. It is written in Chinese; you can tell your AI which language to use for its responses.

## Get started

1. Choose an AI platform and an available model.
2. Copy the entire [prompt](PROMPT.md) into a new conversation or project.
3. Share your research interests, preferred regions, intended start year, funding requirements and CV. You can provide these gradually.
4. Return during the application cycle to review results and decide what happens next.

## Model and platform setup

Use ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Kimi, DeepSeek or another tool that can handle a long prompt. Choose the model in your platform; the prompt cannot change it for you. If available, enable web research and file reading. Email connections and scheduled tasks are optional and require platform support, configuration and your authorization.

Capabilities, limits and costs depend on the service. Without web access, provide sources for the AI to organize and verify the important details yourself.

Review outreach before sending. Your research questions, applications and final decisions remain yours.
